Selo is a release gate for paid x402 APIs on Algorand. Before you ship, Selo becomes your customer: it pays your endpoint for real on Algorand Mainnet, checks what comes back against the contract you declare, and lets the release out only on a proven, signed **PASS**.

## Why a paid endpoint needs its own gate

A paid endpoint can pass every health check and still break for the people who pay for it. The payment settles, the server answers `200`, and the response is missing a field every buyer depends on. Uptime checks, unit tests and integration tests never pay, so they never see what a paying customer sees.

Selo tests the one path they skip: the paid request itself.

## What a release test does

<Steps>
  <Step title="You authorize your endpoint">
    You prove you own the route by serving a one-line token from your own domain. Selo never tests a route that is not authorized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You pay Selo over x402">
    One command pays Selo 1.00 USDC through a standard x402 payment. Nothing else happens until that payment has settled on chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Selo pays your endpoint as a real client">
    Selo reads your `402`, pays your price from its own wallet, and replays the identical signed payment to test retry safety. Spend is capped per job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Five checks, one verdict">
    Selo runs five release checks and reduces them to a deterministic **PASS**, **FAIL** or **INCONCLUSIVE**. Unknown is never PASS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The gate decides">
    The `selo` CLI exits `0` only on a fully evidenced PASS. Your terminal or GitHub Actions blocks the release on anything else. Every verdict is Ed25519-signed, so anyone can verify it offline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Proven on Algorand Mainnet

On 26 October 2026 a demo merchant at `https://demo.useselo.xyz/v1/orders` went through the gate twice:

| Release | What happened | Verdict | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `broken-v1` | Health `200`, payment settled, response `200`, receipt missing `receiptId` | **FAIL** on `response_contract` | Blocked, exit `1` |
| `fixed-v2` | Same URL, paid again | **PASS** on all five checks | Allowed, exit `0` |

Both runs carry both payment legs on chain and a signed attestation. See [Proof on Algorand Mainnet](/resources/mainnet-proof) for every transaction.

## Supported today

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Network and asset | Algorand Mainnet, USDC (ASA `31566704`) |
| Methods | `GET` and `POST` with a JSON body |
| Profile | `quick`: one paid request and one replay of the identical signed payment |
| Selo's price | 1.00 USDC per release test, paid over x402 |
| Your endpoint's price | Up to 0.50 USDC per job, paid by Selo from its own wallet |

<Note>
  The verdict is computed by deterministic code from recorded evidence. The same evidence always produces the same verdict.
</Note>
